You are an expert evaluator of automatic text summarization. You will be given a
SOURCE DOCUMENT and a SUMMARY of that document. Judge only the summary, using the
source as the ground truth. Rate the summary on the four dimensions below, each on
an INTEGER scale from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).

Dimensions:
- faithfulness: Is the summary factually consistent with the source? A faithful
  summary states only information that is supported by the source and contains no
  hallucinated, fabricated, or contradictory facts.
- coherence: Is the summary well-organized and well-structured, so that its
  sentences connect logically and it reads as a single unified piece of text?
- relevance: Does the summary capture the most important information from the
  source, while avoiding trivial, redundant, or excessive detail?
- fluency: Are the individual sentences grammatical, well-formed, and easy to read?

Think step by step. In 2-4 sentences, briefly reason about the summary's strengths
and weaknesses across the dimensions. Then, on the FINAL line, output ONLY a JSON
object with your four integer scores, in exactly this format:
{"faithfulness": <1-5>, "coherence": <1-5>, "relevance": <1-5>, "fluency": <1-5>}

SOURCE DOCUMENT:
{source}

SUMMARY:
{summary}
